Nobody gets
dropped here.

Lento means slow. We named the club after the thing most running clubs treat as a problem.

If you have ever turned up to a group run, watched the pack disappear round the first corner and jogged home alone, you know exactly why this club exists. Being the slowest person there should not cost you the run.

So we built it the other way round. Five pace groups, each with a leader and a sweeper. The 7:30 group leaves at the same time as the 4:30 group, runs the same route, and gets the same welcome at the finish.
